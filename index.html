01 / ABOUT

Physics across
scales and symmetries.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at CNRS / IPCMS in France, working with Giovanni Manfredi.

My research sits at the intersection of condensed matter theory, spintronics, and scientific computing. I study magnetic skyrmions, spin waves, and altermagnetic textures—from their fundamental dynamics to their potential roles in memory, neuromorphic devices, and quantum information.

Available after completion of my current postdoctoral position in 2027.
