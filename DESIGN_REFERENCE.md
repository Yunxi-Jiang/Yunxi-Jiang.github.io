## Adapted implementation prompt

> Build a refined, dark-theme academic portfolio for Yunxi Jiang, a condensed matter physicist and postdoctoral researcher at CNRS/IPCMS. Use oversized editorial typography, a live animated skyrmion spin field, restrained lime/cyan/violet accents, generous negative space, rounded research panels, and scroll-reveal motion. Organize the page into hero, research interests, complete publications, academic trajectory, skills, and contact. Keep the tone rigorous and sophisticated rather than commercial. Make every publication link directly to its DOI, arXiv, or repository record, and make the site responsive, accessible, and deployable to GitHub Pages without a build step.
